📱 Access From Your Phone

CURRENT ACCESS URL
http://localhost:8000

To open Trakerz on your Android phone:

  1. Connect your smartphone to the same Wi-Fi network as your laptop.
  2. Find your laptop's Local IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.X).
  3. On Chrome on your phone, navigate to: http://<your-laptop-ip>:8000
  4. Tap Chrome's Menu (⋮) → "Add to Home screen" to install Trakerz like a native mobile app!
